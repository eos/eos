B->Dlnu::P(q2)
    PDF for the decay $\bar{B}\to D \ell^-\bar\nu$ as a function of the invariant dilepton mass squared $q^2$.

    Numerator kinematic variables:q2
    Denominator kinematic variables:q2_min, q2_max

B->Dlnu::P(q2,cos(theta_l))
    PDF for the decay $\bar{B}\to D \ell^-\bar\nu$ as a function of the invariant dilepton mass squared $q^2$
                    and the cosine of the angle $\theta_\ell$ between the charged lepton and the negative $\bar{B}$ flight direction in the $\ell^-\bar\nu$ rest frame.

    Numerator kinematic variables:q2, cos(theta_l)
    Denominator kinematic variables:q2_min, q2_max
